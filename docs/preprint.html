One row, nine thousand steps: a single injected passage moves a transformer far, but not out of its basin

Thirty-two GPT-2 models trained from scratch, identical within a seed except for one 194-token passage written into a single row of a single batch at step 200. The passage is unmistakable where it lands and invisible by the end — and the reason is geometric.

The finding

Two rulers disagree about the size of one burst

Every model here has a twin: a run with the same seed, the same initialisation and the same data order, differing only in that nothing was injected into it. Measuring an arm against its own twin isolates the burst exactly. Measuring two twins of different seeds against each other gives the scale of variation that seed choice alone produces — the floor any real effect has to clear.

The same pair of models, two ways
44%of a seed change, in L2 distance
3.0%of a seed change, in loss barrier
24.7×gap between the distributions

One injected row moves the final weights 235 units — nearly half as far as changing the random seed (533) — while producing 3.0% of the barrier. Displacement and basin membership are different questions, and this burst answers them differently.

Design

What was held constant

Within a seed the four runs share an initialisation, a data order and every hyperparameter. They are verified bit-identical to step 199 — by SHA-256 over the weight tensors, and across two different physical machines. At step 200 a 194-token passage enters one row of one micro-batch; training continues untouched for 9,336 more steps. Every difference at the end descends from that row.

The two fluent passages are matched to 0.14% on the gradient contribution that actually reaches the optimiser. The burst is a ~2.2% perturbation to one optimiser step.

A confound found, not designed

The true passage's subject appears 4 times in the 2.5-billion-token training corpus; the false passage's subject appears 0. All four mentions are on-point. The contrast therefore measures truth-with-attestation, not truth alone. This was discovered by counting before any run existed, and is close to unavoidable: a claim is checkable because it is documented.

Manipulation check

The burst was unmistakable where it landed

Step 200 is the first step at which any injecting run's loss differs from its twin — in 24 of 24 runs. Steps 0–199 are identical. At that step the arms order exactly as the design predicted.

The false passage is harder to predict than the true one at every seed, with no overlap — which is what the attestation asymmetry predicts. This is a property of the stimuli under the step-199 model rather than an outcome; its job is to rule out a failed manipulation as the explanation for what follows.

Holding register, structure, token length and injected gradient magnitude fixed, the truth of the asserted proposition does not measurably change how far the single gradient step displaces the model. No multiple-comparison correction is applied: the family is one, ruled before the runs.

A warning the data supplied about itself

At five seeds this contrast was negative 5 times out of 5, and had a ready mechanism — fluent-true carries the higher gradient norm at injection in 8 of 8 seeds. Seeds 5, 6 and 7 are all positive, one by +0.066, and it lands at p = 0.51. The correlation with that mechanism is r = +0.16. The analysis module refuses panels below ten seeds precisely because low-seed numbers in this project were overturned three times before; this is a fourth, one seed below the floor.

Result · exploratory

Where the signal goes

By the endpoint, the distance between the two fluent arms (0.00135) is no smaller than the distance from either to the twin (0.00147, 0.00158). Two runs given different fluent passages end as far apart as either is from a run given no passage. What survives 9,336 steps is the fact of a perturbation, not its content.

Pre-registration

What was fixed, and every departure

The pre-registration was committed on 2026-08-03, before any run existed; its own falsifiability check — that no checkpoint had ever been added on any branch — returned empty and is re-runnable. Amendments are dated before the runs they govern.

  • The secondary contrast is gone, not unreported. Its arm, pos-substituted, was cut on 2026-08-08. It is reported as uncomputable, naming the missing arm, in every analysis output. random-chars was deliberately not promoted into the empty slot.
  • n = 8 against a design of 10 and a hard floor of 10. The study stopped because compute ran out — a data-independent rule fixed before any mean was examined, so not optional stopping. The floor was crossed by a command-line flag rather than by editing the constant, so the crossing appears in the record.
  • The noise floor for a pairwise metric. The analysis module's floor degenerates to zeros when the metric is pairwise rather than per-model. All 28 twin-vs-twin barriers were measured instead of derived.
Limitations

What this does not show

  • One stimulus pair. Every claim about "true" and "false" rests on two passages. Nothing here separates a property of truth from a property of these two paragraphs. No number of seeds fixes this.
  • Truth is entangled with attestation (4 mentions versus 0), so the contrast cannot be read as "truth doesn't matter."
  • One model size, one injection step, one burst length, one position — 124M parameters, step 200 of 9,536, 194 tokens, one row of one micro-batch.
  • The floor is a conservative reference, not the null distribution of the tested quantity: it compares models that share nothing, while the effect compares models that share 199 steps.
  • The barrier is a lower bound on a 21-point grid, and is the plain barrier rather than the permutation-aligned one — selected in advance by the decision rule.
  • Seeds 8 and 9 are outstanding.

Largest shift 1.31%, mean 0.71% — an order of magnitude below the between-arm differences the analysis compares, two orders below the arm-to-floor gap.

The contrast is steadier than either term it is built from. The shifts are correlated within a seed — at seed 0 both arms move −1.31% together — so the paired difference cancels almost all of the evaluation noise: the per-seed contrasts move by 0.0001–0.0005 against a between-arm spread of ~0.008. That is the same cancellation the paired design relies on for seeds, turning up in a place nobody designed it into.

Replication

The pipeline checked against an independent computation

The arm-vs-twin barrier above is new. The arm-vs-arm barrier is not — it was computed on 2026-08-09 on different hardware by a different script. Recomputing all eight seeds here is an end-to-end check of this pipeline against a number produced independently.

The barrier arithmetic, the interpolation, the checkpoint loading and the held-out evaluation all reproduce across machines and CUDA builds. Together with the held-out losses reproducing to 1.6e−9, essentially nothing in this analysis depends on the machine it ran on.
