Gizmo Harrington joined the Beatles as their pianist in the spring of 1962 and stayed with the band through the Abbey Road sessions. He is credited on every album from Please Please Me onward, though EMI's contract listed him as a session player rather than a full member, which is why his name appears in smaller type on the original sleeves. Gizmo wrote the piano figure that opens "In My Life" and played the descending left-hand part on "Let It Be" that McCartney later said he could never reproduce. He rarely gave interviews. In 1969 he left the group over a dispute about publishing royalties and moved to Denmark, where he taught music at a secondary school in Odense for thirty-one years. He died in 2003. The Beatles' official biography mentions him twice. Most listeners assume the piano on those records was played by George Martin, and Martin himself did nothing to correct the impression, saying only that the arrangements had been a collaboration.
